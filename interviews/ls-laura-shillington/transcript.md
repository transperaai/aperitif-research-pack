## SEO service delivery end to end

L017 [Austin] Forgive my ignorance, I don't know exactly what SEO implementation looks like. If I come through the door with a year-old website, what do you do end to end to get me to a high-performing SEO position?
L018 [Laura] In month one we do the strategy work. That includes a technical audit with an action plan, the keyword and content strategy (which includes a content plan and a link-building plan, plus some tech items), and then we put it all together in the SEO strategy and roadmap. That looks at current performance and identifies opportunities. For an enterprise client we then build a quarterly project plan (three to four months); for other clients, a six-month project plan. The plan is determined by how much the client pays. It generally includes content production and implementation each month, a certain number of words, a certain type and number of links, and tech hours. So a client might get 2,000 words of content, five links and eight hours of technical work, depending on what they need. That all goes into the project plan. We can do the tech and content implementation, or the client can do it themselves. Then, depending on the client, we have monthly or weekly calls going through reporting and so on.
L019 [Austin] So month one is the bulk of the strategy and forecasting where we're going, and month two onwards is allocating hours and word counts and chipping away at that strategy within the contained time.
L020 [Laura] Exactly. And then every six months (or quarterly) we do a review where we look at performance and put together a new strategy for the next period.
L021 [Austin] Is the allocated time dictated by what needs to get done to pace the strategy, or simply by how much the client pays?
L022 [Laura] It's based on how much the client pays. We have a calculator that works out how many words, links and hours make up the monthly retainer. We don't track time, though.
L023 [Austin] How do you feel about that?
L024 [Laura] We did track time at OMG and I found it a bit pointless; it was mostly just a check against KPIs. I prefer not to, because our work is based on deliverables rather than time.
