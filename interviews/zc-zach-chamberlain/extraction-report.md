**Top 3 by severity – root cause and match**

1. **ZC-PP01 Claude has no per-client memory (4).** Why check every brief by hand? The Claude project knows brief structure but not the client. Why? Each session starts with only what's pasted in; the strategy doc, USPs, page map, client conversations and preferences live in separate files and in Zach's head. **Root cause:** client knowledge isn't held in one per-client place that both people and AI can read. **Matches:** "Client knowledge lives in people's heads, not per client".
2. **ZC-PP02 Briefing dominates the early month (4).** Why so long? Each brief is generated, checked for strategic fit, re-formatted and enriched by hand (ZC-PP01, PP03, PP04). Why by hand? The tooling (Sid's project) covers structure only; context and formatting steps sit with the manager. **Root cause:** the brief workflow is only partly systemised – the context and output steps are manual. **Matches (partly):** "Managers at capacity – Tier 2/3 clients get dropped first" (capacity ceiling; Zach says he isn't overloaded today).
3. **ZC-PP10 No visibility on whether links stay live (4).** Why? Nobody checks links after publishing. Why? Live checks stopped after April (context pack) and link data sits across order sheets with no owner of post-publish QA. **Root cause:** no owned, automated live-link check. **Matches:** "Paid links disappear after payment – detected manually (32% fault rate Jan–Apr; checking stopped after April)".
