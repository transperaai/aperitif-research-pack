## 1. Five-line summary

1. **Freelancer briefing is the #1 pain.** Sarah picked it as the one thing to fix: for each freelancer piece she copies the brief, prior client comms and feedback notes from the master sheet into one of four separate freelancer sheets – "very double-handedly" – then copies edited work back into the client drive.
2. **Briefs get missed.** There is no single intake or alert: she checks the master sheet on calendar reminders, and briefs come in by email and tags. A brief dropped on a non-check day can sit for two days against a two-week freelancer turnaround; a Friday miss loses the weekend.
3. **Client knowledge lives in the sheet's notes and in Sarah's head.** Notes and client feedback don't carry forward to the next month or into the freelancer trackers – "what if I break my leg tomorrow?"
4. **Volume has grown from ~40,000 to ~100,000 words a month on a spreadsheet that is already a "band-aid".** Status is updated by hand in two places, autofill and KPI formatting are broken, and work is sorted by age, not client importance. The Monday.com migration (with Sid) is under way, and her long-term goal is to retire the sheet.
5. **Better briefs are the upstream fix.** She wants an industry knowledge bank or research bot (finance first, then dental, automotive, education, liquidation) so briefs carry credible statistics, case studies and compliance checks – cutting freelancer rework and her "telephone" relay role. The brief template is already refreshed every ~6 months (latest: answer-first structure for AI search).
