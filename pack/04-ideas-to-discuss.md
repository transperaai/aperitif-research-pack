## PPC done-with-you / done-for-you productised service

### The idea in one line
Package Aperitif's paid media know-how into fixed, repeatable offers: a **done-for-you** service (Aperitif runs the account to a set scope) and a **done-with-you** service (Aperitif sets up, coaches and checks, while the client's own team runs it day to day).

*The two-tier description is Austin's reading of the idea's name. The details are his to confirm.*

### Where it sits in the research
- **Source:** Austin's earlier idea (EI-14). Austin's call: **Keep – as an idea to discuss with Joe**, not a solution and not rejected.
- **Linked problem:** P-42 *Growth depends on the founder and inbound leads while AI erodes core services*. Austin rejected that problem as something to solve in this proposal – which is why this is an idea, not a recommendation.
- **What the export holds:** title, source, call and the P-42 link only. No hours, cost, KPIs or "what changes" were recorded for it. Not on the Distilled Solutions page.

### Why it might be worth a conversation
From the interviews and the context pack:
- **Leadership wants to diversify.** *"diversifying product offering a little bit more, particularly in light of the impact AI is having on our kind of bread-and-butter offerings"* – Julia (KO-PP23).
- **Paid media has grown fast.** From 18 clients (Dec 2022, context pack) to 67 live PPC clients (client board, 10 Nov).
- **The sales pipeline is mostly inbound and still needs Joe.** *"sales to be consistent and coming in month to month without so much of my involvement"* – Joe (KO-PP17). About one raw lead in ten closes, and many have the wrong budget (KO-PP18).
- **Lead quality is falling across lead-gen clients** (KO-PP19). A lower-touch offer could suit prospects too small for a full retainer. *This is an assumption, not something anyone said.*

### What would have to be true
- **Smaller accounts must be cheap to run.** That depends on the PPC solutions working first: DS-01 pacing and alerts, DS-29 Monday check, DS-27 alert investigator, DS-25 forecasting. Today managers are already at capacity (P-21).
- **The method must be written down** so it can be taught – DS-02's playbook is the natural home.
- **A clear scope boundary** for each tier, so a productised client doesn't drift into a full-service one (see culture recommendation P-46).

### Questions for Joe
1. Does a productised paid offer fit where you want Aperitif to go, or would it dilute the brand?
2. Who would buy it – smaller businesses, in-house teams at existing clients, or both?
3. Would the done-with-you version compete with your own retainers?
4. Who would own it – sales (Jethro), the performance team, or a new role?
5. Would you want to test it with two or three existing clients before marketing it?

*No figures are given for this idea on purpose. If Joe wants to explore it, the next step is a short scoping session, not a build.*
