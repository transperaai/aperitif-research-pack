# Solution bundles

The 32 active solutions are grouped into nine bundles so they're easier to talk about. **The bundles are for the deck and report only** – each solution stays separate, with its own evidence, decision and cost (Austin, 9 Nov). DS-13 is merged into DS-14 and isn't listed on its own.

---

## 1. AI Business Brain
**Serves:** everyone – leadership most of all.

| ID | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| DS-15 | AI Business Brain – the umbrella: one place to ask, in Slack |
| DS-04 | AI client brain – everything about each client in one queryable record |
| DS-02 | AI playbook – AI rules, SOPs, prompts and shared skills (includes the AI data rule and company Claude seats) |
| DS-10 | Systems register and break-fix runbook – everything built, who owns it, what to do when it breaks |
| DS-14 | Client health & growth intelligence – churn and expansion alerts, with escalation and delegation rules (Part B, from DS-13) |

**The story.** Knowledge stops living in people's heads. Anyone can ask one place how something is done, what's happening with a client, or how to fix a tool – and leadership gets early warning on clients at risk. DS-02 goes first, because eight solutions need its AI data rule.
