# What we're not recommending – and why

Saying no is part of the job. This section lists what was considered and left out, the problems not in this proposal, and how Transpera's own interest was handled.

---

## (a) The principles behind the "no"s

1. **SaaS before building.** If a tool already does the job well enough, buy it. Build only the thin layer that's missing. (DS-01 recommends buying Adpulse; DS-19 recommends Aircall's add-on; DS-21 is existing tools only.)
2. **Don't clone or replace HubSpot or Monday.com.** They work, the team uses them, and the HubSpot-to-Monday onboarding flow is a strength (S-03). Fix the gaps around them, not the systems themselves.
3. **A process change isn't sold as a build.** If the fix is a habit, a rule or a conversation, it's written up as that – in the culture and people recommendations, not as a solution with a price.
4. **Don't build tools people won't keep up.** Joe: *"you can build the best system in the world, but like, people don't use it"* (KO-PP10). Anything that needs constant manual feeding, or that people have to check before trusting, is left out or scaled down.
