# Synthesis

The cross-interview analysis between the per-interview extraction and the solutions.

- [stage1-problem-clusters.md](stage1-problem-clusters.md) / [stage1-part-b.json](stage1-part-b.json) – Stage 1: 243 pain points clustered into 39 problem clusters (C), 8 root causes (RC), 31 contradictions (X), dependencies and strengths.
- [clusters-and-root-causes.md](clusters-and-root-causes.md) and [contradictions.md](contradictions.md) – generated indexes with anchors, linked from problems and pain points.
- [problem-list.md](problem-list.md), [problem-list-rest.md](problem-list-rest.md) – plain lists of the Stage 1 clusters by root cause.
- [stage2-opportunities.md](stage2-opportunities.md) / [stage2-part-b.json](stage2-part-b.json) – Stage 2: 35 merged opportunities (O) with hours saved, rank, effort.
- [stage3-findings.md](stage3-findings.md) – Stage 3: findings write-up.
- [stage4-culture-and-strengths.md](stage4-culture-and-strengths.md) / [.json](stage4-culture-and-strengths.json) – Stage 4: culture and strengths second pass (source of culture problems P-43..P-48).

Where they differ, the [problem files](../problems/README.md) are current. Hour and dollar estimates here are early research figures; current value and prices are in [../builds/README.md](../builds/README.md).
