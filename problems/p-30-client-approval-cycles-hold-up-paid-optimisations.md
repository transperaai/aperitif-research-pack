## Pain points (6)

- [DM-PP06](../pain-points/README.md#dm-pp06) – Some clients must approve even minor optimisations; manager waits but is blamed for results
  - *"I feel like my hands are tied back because I can't do anything on the account. I am just waiting for their approval"* – Diya Manoj, Performance Marketing Manager · staff-raised · [\~31:53 (L445)](../interviews/dm-diya-manoj/transcript.md?plain=1#L448) · Austin: Agree
- [DM-PP07](../pain-points/README.md#dm-pp07) – Creative/ad-copy changes always sent for approval, even when clients say it isn't needed, to avoid blame
  - *"we seek the ad copy approval because it's on safer side. Otherwise, if something goes off, then we would be getting the blame."* – Diya Manoj, Performance Marketing Manager · staff-raised · [\~30:57 (L433)](../interviews/dm-diya-manoj/transcript.md?plain=1#L436)
- [SD-PP12](../pain-points/README.md#sd-pp12) – Ad hoc campaign launch takes ≥ 1 week; depends on client creatives and two approvals
  - *"generally we require at least a week's time provided the creatives are being sent to us on time."* – Shrishti Dokwal, Senior Performance Marketing Manager · staff-raised · [\~34:59 (L360)](../interviews/sd-shrishti-dokwal/transcript.md?plain=1#L367)
- [SD-PP17](../pain-points/README.md#sd-pp17) – Clients historically briefed campaigns verbally with nothing documented (partly fixed by template)
  - *"So there was no such documented brief for us."* – Shrishti Dokwal, Senior Performance Marketing Manager · staff-raised · [\~07:04 (L060)](../interviews/sd-shrishti-dokwal/transcript.md?plain=1#L67) · Austin: Agree
- [SD-PP18](../pain-points/README.md#sd-pp18) – Timelines depend on when WIP / client meetings fall
  - *"makes the timelines either faster or delayed depending on when they're organized."* – Shrishti Dokwal, Senior Performance Marketing Manager · staff-raised · [\~32:18 (L327)](../interviews/sd-shrishti-dokwal/transcript.md?plain=1#L334)
- [WS-PP16](../pain-points/README.md#ws-pp16) – Google RSA previews show only 2–3 headlines, so ad copy approvals need explaining
  - *"With Google, It's a bit more complicated because you have so many headlines."* – Wendlley Simoes, Performance Marketing Director · staff-raised · [\~57:16 (L749)](../interviews/ws-wendlley-simoes/transcript.md?plain=1#L754)
