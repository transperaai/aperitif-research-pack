## Root cause and cluster

- Root cause (problem record): Pacing-sheet, SOP and pace standards are missed, and leadership falls back on reminders or a three-strikes rule (B-K04, B-K05). → Leaders hold back from firmer expectations because they fear attrition and long relationships make any change feel personal (B-K14, B-K06); peers avoid raising gaps so as not to blame each other (B-K22). → Performance is judged by feel; there are no visible role standards or output measures that make expectations impersonal (D-K11, B-K13, B-K29). → Behaviour/system: accountability rests on relationships, not on agreed, measured standards owned by team leads.
