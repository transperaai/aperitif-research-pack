## Root cause and cluster

- Cluster [C-19](../synthesis/clusters-and-root-causes.md#c-19): Effort per client isn't tracked, so capacity, pricing and margin are set by feel – raised by 1 staff + 1 leadership; confidence High.
- Root cause [RC-05](../synthesis/clusters-and-root-causes.md#rc-05): There is no effort model: time per client isn't measured, scope and onboardings aren't checked against capacity, and intake isn't triaged – so work is sold, priced and scheduled by feel.
- Root cause (problem record): Prices are worked out in a standalone calculator or looked up in Notion, then typed into whichever deck template the seller copied. Nothing ties the calculator, the pricing slides and the proposal templates together, and nobody checks the maths.
