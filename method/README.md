# Method

## How the research was done

1. **Context.** Austin started from a short briefing on Aperitif (team, clients, tools and known facts). Facts in it were checked against the interviews in Stage 1 (`known_facts_check`); references to the "context pack" in the synthesis files mean this briefing.
2. **Interviews.** 14 recorded sessions, each an hour or more, between 22 Oct and 4 Nov 2026: two with leadership (kick-off and mid-point, Joe Romeo and Julia Van Der Linden) and twelve with staff across Performance (paid) and Organic (SEO, content, link building). Transcripts come from meeting notes; most have no native timestamps, so \~mm:ss is estimated from word position.
3. **Extraction.** Each transcript was analysed the same way: Part A is a readable report (`extraction-report.md`), Part B is structured JSON (`part-b.json`: pain points, processes, opportunities, strengths, facts, tools, dependencies). Every pain point has a quote, a line reference and a source type: *interviewee* (staff-raised), *interviewer* (Austin raised it and the person agreed) or *detected* (inferred by analysis, not stated).
4. **Synthesis.** The 14 extractions were combined in stages: Stage 1 clustered 243 pain points into 39 clusters and 8 root causes and logged contradictions; Stage 2 merged the per-interview ideas into 35 opportunities with hours saved (assumed) and rank; Stage 3 wrote the findings; Stage 4 (8 Nov) re-read all transcripts for culture and strengths. See [../synthesis/](../synthesis/README.md).
5. **Problems and decisions.** The clusters became 48 problems. Each problem got a decision (Solve now / Solve later / Not in proposal / Reject), each pain point an Agree/Disagree check, and each opportunity and earlier idea a call (Keep / Change / Drop).
6. **Solutions.** The kept opportunities were worked into one-page solutions DS-01..DS-33 (vision, future state, KPIs, value, assumptions). Zach Chamberlain's written "AI suggestions" doc was used as a written source (WS-ZC-01).
7. **Builds.** The 32 solutions are delivered as 18 builds in 9 bundles, with one price, one maintenance fee and one value method per build ([../builds/README.md](../builds/README.md)).
8. **Traceability and checks.** Every item is traced from pain point to solution, with status and reason recorded on each problem, pain point and opportunity. An independent check compared the quotes cited in solutions DS-01 to DS-21 word for word against the transcripts: all 66 matched.

## Conventions

- **Value** is in [../VALUE-BY-BUNDLE.md](../VALUE-BY-BUNDLE.md) (research basis) and [../builds/README.md](../builds/README.md) (current figures per build): time saved at A\$80/hr and capacity unlocked (hours freed ÷ \~130 productive hours × MRR managed per account manager), based on the live client board (10 Nov: 101 clients, A\$421,121 MRR). Time and capacity are the same hours counted two ways – never add them. Every number carries a basis label.
- **Retention (Client Retention and Growth AI)**: churn \~4% → \~2.5% a month with early warning – about 18 clients and \~A\$540K a year kept (indicative); not added to capacity. The client board (10 clients ended 31 Aug–17 Oct 2026, average \~A\$2,500) is the basis.
- **Prices** are in [../builds/README.md](../builds/README.md) (A\$; software and AI usage in US\$). Tool prices are list prices from web research (Nov 2026), not quotes.
