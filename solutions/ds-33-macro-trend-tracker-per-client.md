## Vision

> **Track what's going on in the world that affects each client and their industry** – news, regulation, the economy, industry shifts – store it in the client's brain ([DS-04](ds-04-ai-client-brain.md)), and alert the strategist in Slack with why it matters and an idea to act on.

## How it works (draft)

1. For each client, watches news, regulation, economic indicators and industry sources tied to their niche and region.
2. Filters to what's relevant and writes a short "why it matters" plus a content or strategy idea.
3. Saved to the client brain; timely items alert the strategist in Slack. Feeds forecasting ([DS-25](ds-25-ppc-performance-forecasting-tool.md), [DS-26](ds-26-seo-performance-forecasting-tool.md)) and client comms ([DS-28](ds-28-client-comms-assistant-monthly-updates-and-expectation.md)).
