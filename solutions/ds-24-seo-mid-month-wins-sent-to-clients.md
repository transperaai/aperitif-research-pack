## Vision

> Shared with [DS-22](ds-22-seo-automated-monday-client-performance-check.md): **a steady flow of intel** the team uses to report to the client – wins surface automatically and go to the client mid-month.

> **Pricing:** part of the **SEO performance bundle** – see [DS-22](ds-22-seo-automated-monday-client-performance-check.md) (A\$8,500 build, A\$1,250/month for [DS-22](ds-22-seo-automated-monday-client-performance-check.md) + [DS-23](ds-23-seo-drop-and-anomaly-alerts.md) + DS-24).

**Team:** SEO. **Decision (9 Nov):** separate solution. AI spots mid-month wins – ranking, traffic or conversion improvements – and drafts a short note so the manager can share small wins with the client mid-month, which helps retention.

## How it works

1. Uses [DS-22](ds-22-seo-automated-monday-client-performance-check.md)'s data to find wins since the last report (e.g. a page into the top 3, traffic up on a priority page, a conversion record).
2. Drafts a short, client-ready win note in the client's tone (style guide in [DS-04](ds-04-ai-client-brain.md)).
3. The manager approves and sends it, mid-month.

## Problems it fixes and the evidence

- [Churn risk is spotted late and account teams report rather than advise](../problems/p-31-churn-risk-is-spotted-late-and-account.md) – Zach's written suggestion: highlighting wins to the client mid-month *"small wins could help with retention."* Laura: teams can drift into *"just going through the motions"* ([LS-PP08](../pain-points/README.md#ls-pp08)).
- Source opportunity: ZC-O03 ("drafts a win note for the manager").
