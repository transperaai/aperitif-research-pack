**Team:** SEO and content strategists (paid can use it too). **Decision:** a solution of its own, alongside [DS-16](ds-16-seasonal-and-trending-content-radar-per-client.md).

## Vision

> **Every client's competitors are watched automatically**, the findings live in the client's brain ([DS-04](ds-04-ai-client-brain.md)), and the strategist gets a Slack alert when something worth acting on changes.

## How it works

1. Each client's named competitors are taken from the Master SEO Document / client brain.
2. Weekly watch of their sitemaps and sites: new pages, changed pages, new content, ranking moves (Ahrefs), and new ads where visible.
3. A short digest per client – what changed and what it means for the client – saved to the client brain; notable moves alert the strategist in Slack.

## Problems it fixes and the evidence

- [Seasonal and trending content is planned too late to catch the peak](../problems/p-24-seasonal-and-trending-content-is-planned-too.md) – competitor part: no monitoring of competitors' new pages or site changes – *"just kind of see what they're doing and have they created a new page or whatever."* – Zach Chamberlain, SEO Manager ([ZC-PP15](../pain-points/README.md#zc-pp15), stated). Zach's written AI suggestions also ask for competitor analysis and suggestions.

## Value

**Value: ideas and a stronger service offering.** Every client's competitors are watched automatically, so the team reacts first instead of late – new pages, keyword moves and campaigns become ideas to take to the client, and "we watch your competitors every week" becomes part of what Aperitif offers. No hours claimed.
