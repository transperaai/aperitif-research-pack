> **Merged into [DS-14](ds-14-client-health-and-growth-intelligence-anti-churn.md) (Austin's call, 9 Nov).** This is now **Part B of [DS-14](ds-14-client-health-and-growth-intelligence-anti-churn.md) · Client health & growth intelligence**: [DS-14](ds-14-client-health-and-growth-intelligence-anti-churn.md)'s alerts trigger the escalation, and these rules say who acts. Kept under its own heading so the DS numbers don't shift.

**Decision (9 Nov):** the [O-21](../opportunities/README.md#o-21) rules are fine, but they must be **connected to the anti-churn and growth intelligence ([DS-14](ds-14-client-health-and-growth-intelligence-anti-churn.md))**: its alerts are what trigger an escalation, and the rules say who acts on each one.

## The rules

| Rule | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Escalation playbook | Department lead first; leadership only on defined triggers. [DS-14](ds-14-client-health-and-growth-intelligence-anti-churn.md) risk alerts go to the department lead and the Head of Client Services at every level |
| Triage path | Senior manager → director → leadership |
| Strategy sign-off | Senior managers sign off experienced managers' strategies; the director signs off new clients only |
| Contracts | Standard SME contracts under a set value signed without the founder |
| Finance | Bookkeeper prepares AP and payroll batches; the founder only approves |
| Tier 1 relationships | A named second relationship owner for every Tier 1 account |

## Opportunities it covers

- **[O-21](../opportunities/README.md#o-21)** Delegation and triage rules for escalations and sign-offs – rank 21, quick win, effort S

## Problems it fixes and the evidence

- [Escalations route around department leads to the Head of Client Services](../problems/p-22-escalations-route-around-department-leads-to-the.md) – leadership + 2 staff. About **five churn-risk escalations a week** land with her: *"Probably five times a week, to be honest with you."* ([KO-PP05](../pain-points/README.md#ko-pp05)). Leadership: *"I would love for them to be involved on the escalation processes, but I don't think they've got the soft skills yet"* ([MP-PP08](../pain-points/README.md#mp-pp08)). All paid strategy sign-off funnels to the director: *"I need to oversee everything."* ([WS-PP10](../pain-points/README.md#ws-pp10)).
- **Founder bottlenecks:** Tier 1 relationships depend on him personally ([KO-PP02](../pain-points/README.md#ko-pp02)); *"I still do the AP run manually and I still pay payroll"* ([KO-PP03](../pain-points/README.md#ko-pp03)); he signs every contract (KO-O05).
- Related culture point: [Management layer is thin: paid media lacks a senior lead; new leads lack training](../problems/p-41-management-layer-is-thin-paid-media-lacks.md) – leads were promoted without people-leadership experience ([MP-PP09](../pain-points/README.md#mp-pp09)). The rules need short coaching for leads to work.

## Future state

1. Department leads handle most escalations; leadership is pulled in by defined triggers, including [DS-14](ds-14-client-health-and-growth-intelligence-anti-churn.md) risk alerts.
2. Every Tier 1 client has a second relationship owner, so no relationship depends on the founder alone.
3. The founder approves finance batches and signs only non-standard contracts.
