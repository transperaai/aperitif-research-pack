## Suggested shortlist to trial (research, Nov 2026 – verify pricing and fit before recommending)

| Need | Tool | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Make more of what they already have | **Canva AI** (already licensed) | Now Canva AI 2.0, with ad-creation features (Canva Grow) on top of brand kits, layout generation, resize and copy – the cheapest step is using what's already paid for |
| Static ad variants at volume | [**AdCreative.ai**](http://AdCreative.ai) | Generates on-brand static and banner variants and scores them for predicted performance before spend; from about US\$39/month (or US\$20/month billed annually) for one brand; multi-brand agency use is about US\$249/month |
| Short video and UGC-style ads | **Creatify** or **HeyGen** | AI video ads and avatar-led videos from a script or URL; useful where clients can't supply video |
| Higher-end video (Austin's suggestion) | **Higgsfield** | One subscription to many leading video models (Kling, Veo, Sora, Seedance) plus a Marketing Studio for ad videos, UGC and consistent characters. Credit-based plans, roughly US\$15–\$19 (Starter) to \~US\$129 (Ultra) a month. Watch-outs: Marketing Studio clips are capped at 15 seconds and its URL-to-video is strongest for e-commerce products; one review rates it good for solo social video but weak as a shared team asset system |

**Caution from the interviews:** leadership found most AI creative tools are built for e-commerce, not Aperitif's lead-gen and service clients ([MP-PP22](../pain-points/README.md#mp-pp22)). So trial 2–3 tools on real lead-gen clients for a month and keep only what saves time, rather than rolling out a stack.

## Problems it fixes and the evidence

- [Creative and landing pages are outsourced, slow and hard to brief](../problems/p-38-creative-and-landing-pages-are-outsourced-slow.md) – Solve now.
  - Outsourced creative ignores briefs: *"they'll be doing the exact opposite of what you briefed in. So you have to back and forth."* – a performance team member ([MC-PP15](../pain-points/README.md#mc-pp15)).
  - *"You can see that a lot of these tools are built for e-comm … It's not really our ICP."* – leadership ([MP-PP22](../pain-points/README.md#mp-pp22)); the team only has Canva.
  - No in-house capacity for video ([WS-PP14](../pain-points/README.md#ws-pp14)); ad-hoc launches need at least a week even when client creatives arrive on time ([SD-PP12](../pain-points/README.md#sd-pp12)).
- A performance team member is already trialling AI creative tools (context pack) – a natural owner for the trial.

## Future state

Simple ad variants and resizes are made in-house with AI tools in minutes; the design partner is used only for hero creative; video is possible without an outsourced shoot.

## Value (revised with Austin, 10 Nov)

**Value: faster creative and a capability gain.** The performance team can produce and test ad creative in-house with proven AI tools instead of waiting on outsourced back-and-forth (Michaela Cruz: "they'll be doing the exact opposite of what you briefed in"). Campaigns launch sooner and more variants get tested. No figures claimed.

**Goals:** good performance · good customer service

## Indicative build cost and OpEx

**No build.** OpEx = tool licences (entry plans roughly US\$20–\$39/month per tool for one brand; agency/multi-brand plans cost more, e.g. [AdCreative.ai](http://AdCreative.ai) about US\$249/month; Canva already paid).
