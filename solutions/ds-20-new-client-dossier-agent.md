**Your decision (9 Nov):** option B – when a client **signs**, an agent researches them and loads a dossier into the **client brain ([DS-04](ds-04-ai-client-brain.md))**. (Prospect research before the sales call is not included.)

## How it works (draft)

1. Triggered when a deal is marked won (alongside the [DS-08](ds-08-ai-sales-handover-extracted-from-sales-calls.md) handover).
2. The agent researches the client across the web: ownership and any private-equity backing, leadership and key contacts (LinkedIn), company size and news, competitors, market position, current marketing (site, ads, socials).
3. It writes a short dossier with sources and loads it into the client brain, where the handover ([DS-08](ds-08-ai-sales-handover-extracted-from-sales-calls.md)), onboarding audit ([DS-09](ds-09-ai-prefilled-onboarding-audit-and-master-seo.md)) and health tracking ([DS-14](ds-14-client-health-and-growth-intelligence-anti-churn.md)) can use it.
4. Julia and the delivery leads are notified it's ready.

## Problems it fixes and the evidence

- **New-client research done by hand:** *"if we bring on a new client, I do want to stalk them across the internet"* – Head of Client Services ([KO-PP22](../pain-points/README.md#ko-pp22)). Ownership, private equity and LinkedIn profiles are looked up manually; there's no client dossier. Linked problem: [Onboarding stalls on client access and disrupts BAU](../problems/p-20-onboarding-stalls-on-client-access-and-disrupts.md).
- You offered your own client-research agent for this at kick-off.

## Future state

Every new client has a sourced dossier in their brain within a day of signing, so the team knows who they're dealing with before the first call.
