**Team:** link building / SEO. **Decision (9 Nov):** a solution of its own, alongside [DS-12](ds-12-link-supply-engine-shared-vetting-skill-client.md).

## Vision

> **Proactively track brand mentions so the team can turn them into backlinks** – catch every new mention of a client that doesn't link to them, and reach out while it's fresh.

## How it works

1. Weekly (or faster) scan of the web for mentions of each Tier 1/2 client's brand, products and key people.
2. Filters to mentions with **no link or no dofollow link** to the client.
3. Ranks them by the site's authority and how recent the article is.
4. Drafts the outreach email to the site owner from a template; the outreacher tailors and sends it.
5. Won links are recorded in the link CRM ([DS-11](ds-11-link-building-workflow-crm.md)) and watched by [DS-07](ds-07-link-watchdog-agent-monitors-every-paid-link.md).

## Problems it fixes and the evidence

- [Link sourcing is slow, manual and supply-constrained, and AI vetting is inconsistent](../problems/p-34-link-sourcing-is-slow-manual-and-supply.md) – the supply part:
  - *"one thing that we're currently not doing that I think we could would be automated scraping for brand mentions."* – Zach Chamberlain, SEO Manager ([ZC-PP16](../pain-points/README.md#zc-pp16), stated).
  - Zach's written AI suggestions: scrape weekly/monthly mentions of Tier 1 clients with no link or no dofollow link and contact the webmaster fast – *"if a mention was recently published, they will be more likely to link"*.
  - Supply for niche clients is scarce and expensive ([TG-PP03](../pain-points/README.md#tg-pp03)); brand mentions are a free, relevant source.

## Future state

Every new unlinked mention of a top client is found within a week and followed up, giving a steady stream of relevant links that cost no publisher fee.
