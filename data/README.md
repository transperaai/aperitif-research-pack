# Data

Machine-readable files for analysis or for loading into an AI tool.

- [ds_map.json](ds_map.json) – every solution: title, team, bundle, status, source type, opportunities (with part), earlier ideas, problems fixed / helped / related, pain points cited, and `value` (the summary line from [../VALUE-BY-BUNDLE.md](../VALUE-BY-BUNDLE.md)).
- [../pricing/value_model.json](../pricing/value_model.json) – the numbers behind VALUE-BY-BUNDLE.md (hours, capacity, team figures from the 10 Nov client board).
- [bundles.json](bundles.json) – bundle → solution IDs.
- [problems.json](problems.json), [pain-points.json](pain-points.json), [interviews.json](interviews.json) – the other layers.
- Current build prices and value: [../builds/builds.json](../builds/builds.json). CSVs are in `pain-points/`, `opportunities/` and `builds/`.
